Refund Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Credits are prepaid and are used for the optional paid features: AI motions, AI background removal and automatic reactions. There are two different cases: credits returned automatically when a job does not deliver, and money refunded for unused credits.

1. Credits returned automatically

The credit price of a job is shown before you confirm it and is deducted when the job is created. We return those credits to your balance automatically, with no request needed, when:

  • the job fails for any reason, including an upload or download error, a timeout, or a rejection by the AI provider's content moderation;
  • the AI provider itself reports the job as cancelled; or
  • you cancel the job before it has been sent to the AI provider.

Not returned: if you cancel a job after it has been sent to the AI provider, the provider keeps generating and charges us for it, so the credits stay spent. The cancel button tells you which case applies before you confirm.

If a job whose credits were returned still has a result waiting at the provider, "fetch again" can retrieve it. A delivered result is paid once, so the same credits are deducted again at that point, and you are asked first. If the fetch fails again, they are returned again.

AI background removal is charged when it runs, and its credits are returned automatically if it fails. Automatic reactions are charged one minute at a time, in advance, while switched on; a minute that has started is not returned.

2. Refunds for unused credits

  • You can ask for a refund of a credit pack within 14 days of purchase. If none of the pack's credits have been used you get a full refund. If some have been used we refund the unused part in proportion.
  • Credits already spent on delivered AI motions, completed background removal or minutes of automatic reactions are not refundable.
  • If we discontinue the Service, unused credits are refunded.
  • If a delivered result is unusable because of a fault in our Service, contact us. We will review it and may return the credits.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method through Polar, our merchant of record. When a refund is processed, the matching credits are removed from your balance. Banks and card issuers decide how long the money takes to appear, usually 5 to 10 business days.

3. How to ask

Email namhk5741@gmail.com from the address of your Virtually account with your order receipt or the date of purchase. We reply within two business days. Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank so we can fix the problem quickly.

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the consumer protection laws of your country.
