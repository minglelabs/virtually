Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what Virtually, operated by MingleLabs ("we", "us"), collects when you use the Service, why, and who else handles it. We do not sell your personal data and we do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

1. What we collect

DataWhy we use it
Your Google account email, name and a Google account identifier, received when you sign inTo identify your account, sign you in, keep your credit balance and contact you about your account
A sign-in session cookieTo keep you signed in. It is a strictly necessary cookie, not used for tracking
Your content: character photos, motion and reference videos, generated clipsTo run the overlay, the motion library and AI motions for you
Credit ledger: balance, purchases, charges, refunds and top-upsTo account for credits and handle refunds and support. Kept as accounting records
Activity log: which account created, uploaded, deleted or played something, and whenSecurity, abuse prevention, support and troubleshooting
Order information received from Polar (order, checkout and customer identifiers, email, and the credits granted)To credit your purchase and process refunds

We do not receive your card number. Payment details are entered on Polar's checkout.

2. Who else handles your data

  • Google – sign-in.
  • Polar (polar.sh) – merchant of record: payment, tax, receipts and refunds. Polar is an independent controller of the payment data it collects, under its own privacy policy.
  • Railway – the hosting provider that runs our servers and stores the data above.
  • AI video providers – when you create an AI motion, we send the provider that runs the job the character image (placed on a plain background) and the reference video (re-encoded and trimmed), plus a text prompt describing the motion. We currently use WaveSpeed AI. The software also supports fal.ai, Higgsfield, Replicate and Kling, which we may use in the future; we will update this page if so. These providers process the files under their own terms and policies and may store the files and results for a limited time, and a result may be reachable by its link at the provider until it expires (for example, about 7 days at WaveSpeed). Do not send content you are not willing to share with them.

We also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

3. How long we keep it

Your content stays until you delete it in the app or ask us to delete your account. Credit ledger and order records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and dispute handling. Activity logs are kept as long as needed for security and support. Providers keep data according to their own retention policies.

4. Your choices

You can delete characters, photos and motions in the app. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to close your account, by emailing namhk5741@gmail.com from your account address. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority.

5. Security

Sign-in is required for the app, the overlay address is a private key link, and credentials are stored with restricted file permissions on the server. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. International processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. By using the Service you understand that your data may be transferred there.

7. Children

The Service is not for people under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this policy as the Service changes and show the date above. Questions: namhk5741@gmail.com.
