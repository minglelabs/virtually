For VTubers and streamers

Your character, live on stream.

Virtually shows your own pre-made 2D character in OBS: as a transparent overlay on top of your live camera, or put together with your own background videos and images into one scene. Press a button on the controller and the character plays a motion once – a wave, a wink, a finger heart – then goes back to idle.

How it works

1

Add your character

Create a character with a name and one or more photos of your own artwork. Pick the photo you want on air.

2

Add motions

Upload a finished motion video you made yourself, or optionally have a new motion clip prepared for your character (see below).

3

Compose your scene

Add your own background videos, images or a frame as layers, put them in order, and size and place your character. Or skip this and use the character as a plain transparent overlay.

4

Go live

Add one Browser Source to OBS. Its address never changes, even when you switch characters. Press motion buttons while you stream.

What you get

Transparent OBS overlay

Your camera stays an ordinary OBS source. The character is a separate transparent Browser Source on top.

Motion buttons

A controller page with one button per motion, including presets such as hi, wink, cheek heart and finger heart.

Characters and photos

Keep several characters, each with several photos. Switch what is on air without touching OBS.

Scene layers

Layer your own looping background videos, images and frames with the character, and send the whole picture to OBS as one source.

Plain backgrounds removed

Artwork on a plain background is cut out automatically so the background does not show on stream. A busy background can be removed with an optional paid AI remover.

Automatic reactions (optional)

Switch it on and Virtually listens to your microphone and plays the motion that fits what you just said. Charged per minute while it is on.

Optional: AI-prepared motions

Motions are prepared ahead of time. Nothing is generated during your stream.

If you do not want to make a motion video yourself, you can create a short motion clip for one of your photos from a reference video of the movement. This step is run by third-party AI video models. Uploading your own finished motion videos does not use credits.

The price of every AI job is shown in credits before you confirm it. Only use artwork and reference videos you have the right to use. Face swaps, deepfakes of real people, content that infringes trademarks or copyrights, and adult content are not allowed – see the Terms of Service.
